Model controls

For each team, projected points = scoring average × (1 − defensive weight) + opponent points allowed × defensive weight. Projected margin = home projected points − away projected points + home advantage. Totals are the sum of projected team points; the home advantage shifts the margin, not the total.

Model outputs are exploratory estimates, not calibrated probabilities or guarantees. Four-game samples can be distorted by mismatches, injuries, opponent quality, tempo and roster changes.
